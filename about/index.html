Victoria German

I’m a Product Design Lead at J.P. Morgan Chase. I feel lucky to have found a profession that balances my knack for visual arts with a passion for financial literacy.

I love photography. All the images you see on this site are my own. You can view some other photos I’ve taken here, but I no longer post as I’ve been boycotting social media in general.

I met my partner the old fashioned way, on a dating app. He is an engineer, we’ve built some cool things together.

We have two daughters and nothing matters more to me. I spend as much time as I can with them, but I would also like to send them to college one day, so I’ll continue to be a working stiff.

At times, I work as a freelance designer. If you ever want to collaborate, you are welcome to get in touch.
